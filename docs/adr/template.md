# ADR-NNN: Title

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Phase:** N

## Context

What forces the decision. Constraints, requirements, and what breaks if nothing
is decided. Facts, not preferences.

## Decision

What was decided, stated so that an implementer can tell whether they are
complying with it.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative, with the reason it was rejected. An alternative with no
genuine appeal was not really an alternative and should not be listed.

## Trade-offs

What this decision costs. Every real decision costs something.

## Consequences

What now becomes true, what becomes harder, and what must be built or measured
as a result.

## Revisit if

The concrete conditions under which this decision should be reopened.
